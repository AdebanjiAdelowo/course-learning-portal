Given a non-negative integer `n`, return the largest integer `r` such that `r * r <= n`. This is the square root of `n` rounded down.

Use integer arithmetic only. Do not use floating-point numbers, `math.sqrt`, `math.isqrt` or the `**` operator with a fractional exponent. Floating-point square roots give wrong answers for large integers, and `n` may be as large as $10^{18}$.

Your solution must run in $O(\log n)$ iterations.
