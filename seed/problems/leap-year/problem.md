Given a year as a positive integer, return `True` if it is a leap year in the Gregorian calendar and `False` otherwise.

The rule has three parts:

- a year divisible by 4 is a leap year,
- except that a year divisible by 100 is not,
- except that a year divisible by 400 is.

So 2024 and 2000 are leap years, and 2023 and 1900 are not.

Return a `bool`. Do not use the `calendar` module.
